## 1. Inventory (done, offline)

`node scripts/perf/inventory.mjs` found 206 call sites, **139 distinct APIs** in `src/` and `web/src/`:
107 `.rpc()` sites (96 distinct RPCs, about 31 read-only and 65 that write), 56 table reads,
35 table writes and 9 edge-function invokes (11 functions exist; `join`, `send-push` and
`razorpay-webhook` are called by others, not the app). 24 call sites have no hook (called from
screens, stores or edge code). Realtime: **none** (no `channel()` or realtime use in the app).

pg_cron (all in migrations): `corefit-reminders` 5 min, `corefit-enquiry-reminders` 5 min,
`corefit-auto-check-out` 10 min, `corefit-birthdays` hourly :05, `corefit-nudges` hourly :15,
`corefit-purge-logs` daily. About 36 triggers; the write-heavy ones are the `notifications`
statement trigger (pg_net to `send-push`), `attendance_worked_minutes`, `memberships_number_receipt`
and the `require_paid_branch` guards.

Full table (type, R/W, api function, hooks, screens): `docs/perf/raw/inventory-2026-11-15.json`, and the
per-API table below. "When" per call is in the JSON's hook; roughly: app start = profile,
`contact_details`, `is_platform_admin`, `my_gym_invites`, `branch_access`, notifications; screen open =
everything else; focus = any query older than the 30 s default staleTime refetches when the app
returns to the foreground; user action = every write.
