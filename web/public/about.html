Android first. iPhone is coming soon; the web dashboard works on any device.

Yes. Fill in our CSV template and import them with their plans and dates.

Straight to your own Razorpay account. CoreFit never holds it and takes no cut.

Every record is locked to its gym and role. Read our privacy policy.
